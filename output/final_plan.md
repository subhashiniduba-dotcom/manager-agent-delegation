# 2-Week Learning Plan
## Week 1  (Day 1 ... Day 7, each with topics and a practice item)

- **Day 1: Welcome to SQL & The `SELECT` Statement**
  - **Topics:** Understanding database tables (rows/columns), basic retrieval using `SELECT` and `FROM`.
  - **Practice Item:** Write a query to view all columns from a `customers` table, then modify it to view just the `first_name` and `email` columns. (Ref: *Drill 1 & 2*)

- **Day 2: Eliminating Clutter with `DISTINCT`**
  - **Topics:** Dealing with duplicate data and requesting unique values.
  - **Practice Item:** Find all the unique countries your customers live in from the `customers` table. (Ref: *Drill 3*)

- **Day 3: Meeting the `WHERE` Clause (Filtering Data)**
  - **Topics:** Filtering rows based on conditions using comparison operators (`=`, `>`, `<`, `<>`).
  - **Practice Item:** Select all products from a `products` table where the price is greater than $50. (Ref: *Challenge 2*)

- **Day 4: Combining Conditions with `AND`, `OR`, and `NOT`**
  - **Topics:** Refining filters with multiple criteria occurring simultaneously or alternatively.
  - **Practice Item:** Find products that cost more than $50 **and** belong to the 'Electronics' category. (Ref: *Challenge 2*)

- **Day 5: Exploring Ranges and Lists with `BETWEEN` and `IN`**
  - **Topics:** Shorthand filtering for numerical ranges and specific value lists.
  - **Practice Item:** Find all orders placed between '2023-01-01' and '2023-01-31', or orders placed in cities 'New York', 'London', or 'Tokyo'. (Ref: *Drill 7, Challenge 4*)

- **Day 6: Pattern Matching with `LIKE` and Wildcards**
  - **Topics:** Searching text using wildcards (`%` and `_`).
  - **Practice Item:** Find all employees or customers whose first names start with the letter 'J' (or last name starts with 'S'). (Ref: *Drill 6*)

- **Day 7: Organizing Your View with `ORDER BY`**
  - **Topics:** Sorting results in ascending (`ASC`) or descending (`DESC`) order, and limiting output.
  - **Practice Item:** List all employees by their salary from highest to lowest, or complete the Week 1 Capstone Challenge (filtering, sorting, and limiting recent active orders). (Ref: *Challenge 1 & Week 1 Capstone*)

## Week 2  (Day 8 ... Day 14, each with topics and a practice item)

- **Day 8: Introduction to Aggregate Functions**
  - **Topics:** Summary statistics across rows using `COUNT()`, `SUM()`, `AVG()`, `MIN()`, and `MAX()`.
  - **Practice Item:** Find the total revenue generated by all orders and calculate the average product price. (Ref: *Drill 8 & 9*)

- **Day 9: Grouping Data with `GROUP BY`**
  - **Topics:** Breaking aggregate calculations down into distinct categories.
  - **Practice Item:** Count how many products belong to each individual category, or find the total revenue generated for each order status. (Ref: *Challenge 5 & 6*)

- **Day 10: Filtering Aggregates with `HAVING`**
  - **Topics:** Differentiating between `WHERE` (filters rows pre-grouping) and `HAVING` (filters groups post-grouping).
  - **Practice Item:** Find categories that have more than 10 products listed in them, or find customers who have placed more than 3 orders. (Ref: *Drill 10, Challenge 7*)

- **Day 11: Introduction to Table Relationships & `INNER JOIN`**
  - **Topics:** Understanding relational databases and gluing tables together using foreign keys and `INNER JOIN`.
  - **Practice Item:** Join the `orders` table with the `customers` table to see each order alongside the customer's name. (Ref: *Drill 11*)

- **Day 12: Expanding Horizons with `LEFT JOIN` and `RIGHT JOIN`**
  - **Topics:** Handling unmatched rows and keeping outer table data visible.
  - **Practice Item:** List all customers, even those who haven't placed an order yet, along with their order IDs if they have. (Ref: *Challenge 9*)

- **Day 13: Joining Multiple Tables & Using Aliases (`AS`)**
  - **Topics:** Writing complex queries connecting three or more tables while maintaining readability via table aliases.
  - **Practice Item:** Join `orders`, `customers`, and products/employees to see who bought what and total customer lifetime spend. (Ref: *Challenge 8 & Mini Challenge 10*)

- **Day 14: Capstone Review & Putting It All Together**
  - **Topics:** Combining `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, `JOIN`, and `ORDER BY` into unified analytics queries.
  - **Practice Item:** Write a comprehensive query that calculates category audit metrics (count, min/max price) for categories containing at least 5 products. (Ref: *Mini Challenge 11*)

## Practice
To cement your learning through hands-on coding, incorporate these structured drills and challenges:
- **Week 1 Focus (Foundations & Filtering):**
  - *Drills:* Column selection (`SELECT`, `FROM`), column renaming/aliases, finding unique values (`DISTINCT`), sorting (`ORDER BY`), basic filtering (`WHERE`, `AND`, `OR`), pattern matching (`LIKE`), and range checks (`BETWEEN`, `IN`).
  - *Week 1 Capstone Drill:* Filter active/completed orders from specific countries with order totals over $100, sorted by date descending and limited to the top 10.
- **Week 2 Focus (Aggregation & Joins):**
  - *Drills:* Aggregate calculations (`COUNT`, `SUM`, `AVG`, `MIN`, `MAX`), categorical grouping (`GROUP BY`), group filtering (`HAVING`), and multi-table relational queries (`INNER JOIN`, `LEFT JOIN`).
  - *Week 2 Capstone Drills:* Calculate customer lifetime spend (filtering for high-spenders $\ge \$1,000$) and build an E-Commerce Category Audit report showing product counts and price ranges for well-stocked categories.
- **Practice Rule:** Always type out queries manually in an interactive SQL environment (like SQLBolt, LeetCode, or HackerRank) rather than copy-pasting solutions.

## Projects
Progress from beginner trivia queries to portfolio-ready analytics using three real-world datasets:
1. **Project 1: The Olympic Historian (Difficulty: Easy — End of Week 1)**
   - *Dataset:* 120 years of Olympic history (Kaggle: `heesoo37/120-years-of-olympic-history-athletes-and-results`).
   - *Skills:* `SELECT`, `WHERE`, `GROUP BY`, `HAVING`, `ORDER BY`, basic `CASE WHEN`.
   - *Deliverable:* A `.sql` file answering historical sports trivia (top gold-medal countries, top sports post-2000, and average height/weight summaries).
2. **Project 2: E-Commerce Customer 360 (Difficulty: Medium — Mid-Week 2)**
   - *Dataset:* Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist (Kaggle).
   - *Skills:* Multi-table `INNER/LEFT JOIN`s, Subqueries, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), Date/Time functions, `COALESCE`.
   - *Deliverable:* A script identifying top customers by spend, analyzing slowest shipping states via CTEs, and preparing Recency-Frequency-Monetary (RFM) metrics.
3. **Project 3: Streaming Service Retention & Trends (Portfolio Capstone — End of Week 2)**
   - *Dataset:* Spotify / Music Streaming Tracks dataset (Kaggle).
   - *Skills:* Advanced Window Functions (`ROW_NUMBER()`, `RANK()`, `LAG()`, `SUM() OVER()`), Self-Joins, Complex CTEs.
   - *Deliverable:* A GitHub repository featuring a clean `README.md`, schema representation, and portfolio-grade queries for track ranking, running totals, and month-over-month user cohort retention analysis.

## Final Advice
- **Commit to a Sustainable Schedule:** Dedicate a focused **2 hours per day** (following the 15-min warm-up, 30-min concept input, 60-min hands-on coding, and 15-min wrap-up structure). Avoid burnout by resisting marathon study sessions.
- **Follow the 70/30 Rule:** Spend 30% of your time reading/watching tutorials and 70% actively typing and debugging queries. 
- **Avoid Tutorial Hell:** Do not just watch videos; predict query syntax, pause often, and solve problems hands-on.
- **Maintain an Error Log:** Document every syntax error and why it occurred, reviewing your log every 3 days.
- **Master Query Logic & NULLs:** Always remember the execution order (`FROM` $\rightarrow$ `WHERE` $\rightarrow$ `GROUP BY` $\rightarrow$ `HAVING` $\rightarrow$ `SELECT` $\rightarrow$ `ORDER BY`) and always use `IS NULL` instead of `= NULL`.
- **Embrace Cumulative Revision:** Use Days 7 and 14 exclusively to review past challenges without looking at previous solutions.
